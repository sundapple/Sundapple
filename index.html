Fishion

Track your catch. Read the water.

Fishion is a simple log for anglers — record every catch with species, size, spot and conditions, then look back on the water that's actually working for you.

  • Log every catch — species, size, spot, and conditions
  • A personal map of your best spots, built as you fish
  • Weather and conditions saved alongside each trip
