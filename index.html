Fishion

Track your catch. Read the water.

Fishion is a simple log for anglers — record every catch with species, size, spot and conditions, then look back on the water that's actually working for you.

  • Log every catch — species, size, spot, and conditions
  • A personal map of your best spots, built as you fish
  • Weather and conditions saved alongside each trip

Inside Fishion

What you actually get

Fishdex

289 species of fish, shark, ray, squid, crab and lobster, sorted rarest first. Each one stays locked until you land it.

Catch log

Start a session and every catch is stamped with the conditions it happened in. The patterns surface on their own.

Find

Every water near you, scored on catch potential and conditions. Sorted so the best one sits at the top.
