Fishion

Track your catch. Read the water.

Fishion is an AI fishing companion. It names what you've caught, points you to the best water nearby, and turns every trip into progress — a Fishdex to fill, a log that spots your patterns, and thrones to claim.

  • Identify any catch from a photo with AI
  • Find nearby waters, ranked by catch potential and conditions
  • Log sessions with conditions saved alongside every catch
  • Unlock 550+ species in the Fishdex and claim thrones in the Arena

Inside Fishion

What you actually get

Identify a catch

Snap a photo and the AI names the species, with facts, nutrition and edibility notes to check against. Earn XP every time.

Find

Every water near you, scored on catch potential and conditions. Sorted so the best one sits at the top.

Fishdex

Over 550 species of fish, shark, ray, squid, crab and lobster, sorted rarest first. Each one stays locked until you land it.

Catch log

Start a session and every catch is stamped with the conditions it happened in. The patterns surface on their own.

Arena

Claim thrones at real piers, beaches and creeks. Scout them anywhere in the world, but you have to be within 300 m to take one.
